THEORETICAL FOUNDATION

The Triad of Islamic Technology

Technology in Islamic thought is neither autonomous nor neutral. It is governed by a tripartite theological matrix rooted in the Quranic worldview.

01

Taskheer (تَسْخِير)

Divine Subjection

Nature is not an enemy to be subjugated or conquered. The cosmos has been graciously subjected by God for human sustenance (Surah Luqman 31:20). Technology is the humble realization of cosmic laws (Sunan Allah).

02

Khilafah (اسْتِخْلاف)

Moral Stewardship

Humankind is not a sovereign conqueror, but a trustee (Khalifah) bound by a sacred trust (Amanah). Technological innovation must preserve cosmic equilibrium (Mizan) and prevent earthly ruin (Fasad fi al-Ard).

03

'Ilm Nafi' (عِلْم نَافِع)

Beneficial Teleology

The Prophet ﷺ prayed for beneficial knowledge and sought refuge from knowledge that does not benefit. Machinery must serve the Maqasid al-Shariah: safeguarding faith, life, intellect, lineage, and public wealth.

DIALECTIC ANALYSIS

Promethean vs. Purposeful Techno-Optimism

Contrasting the Silicon Valley / Western Promethean paradigm with the Islamic purposeful model of Taskheer.

Dimension Western Promethean Techno-Optimism Islamic Purposeful Techno-Realism (Fiqh al-Taskheer)
Origin Mythos Prometheus stealing fire from the gods; human rebellion and total autonomy. The Divine Gift of Taskheer: God subjected the heavens and earth as a sacred trust.
Relation to Nature Nature is an obstacle to be conquered, mined, and remade by sheer human will. Nature is a sacred sign (Ayah) and living balance (Mizan) to be stewarded.
Ultimate Goal (Telos) Technological singularity, transhumanist godhood, and escaping physical limits. Human flourishing (Maslahah), justice ('Adl), and ethical accountability in the Hereafter.
Evaluation Criterion Speed, disruption, commercial efficiency, and venture capital returns. Preservation of the 5 Essentials of Maqasid al-Shariah: Faith, Life, Intellect, Lineage, Wealth.
Labor & Community Creative destruction; displacement of artisans celebrated as inevitable efficiency. Preservation of artisan dignity (Futuwah guilds), equitable wage distribution, and civic welfare.
